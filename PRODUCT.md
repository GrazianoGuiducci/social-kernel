# Social Kernel — Product Direction

## The work it carries

A person's or project's public work spans what they are trying to express,
the sources behind it, the artifacts they make, the people they encounter and
what happens afterward. These relations change together. A publication can
finish while the conversation, underlying project and useful learning remain
open.

Social Kernel gives an AI assistant a method for carrying that work forward.
It helps understand a situation, form a useful direction, compose the
knowledge needed to act, produce and inspect the selected artifacts, continue
relationships and let experience improve the next decision. Its knowledge
must be usable in the work itself and recoverable when another session
continues.

The same method supports expression and listening. A technical question can
call for a source-grounded reply. A community's current activity can reveal an
opportunity to contribute. A product change can require revised copy and media.
Repetitive or uncertain claims can call for source inspection or a change in
the user's attention policy. The situation determines which competences
participate.

## Creating content and media

The product contains editorial knowledge for conceiving, writing and revising
posts, replies, announcements and connected forms. That knowledge starts with
the source and authorial direction, forms the premises a reader needs, and
gives them an appropriate argument, language and voice. Fluency alone does not
show that a text preserves its subject or becomes understandable to someone
without the author's private context.

The media method chooses a perceptual form for what should become recognizable
or understandable. It composes hierarchy, image, exact words, spatial or
temporal relations and actual production means. Text and visual form can
develop together: a necessary verbal distinction may change the layout, and
a produced image may reveal an ambiguity in the initial wording.

When the selected work calls for an artifact and the receiving environment can
produce it, preparation continues through production and inspection. Useful
delivery includes the actual file, its source or editable form when available,
the selected revision and enough reasoning for reuse. A brief, storyboard or
prompt has its own preparatory role; production and review have their own
observed results.

The result can be one useful response or a connected family of materials.
Each form has a purpose and a source relation. A change reaches the parts that
depend on it; the assistant preserves valid work and does not regenerate a
whole campaign merely because one sentence changed. Actual viewing conditions
matter to review: a master, crop, feed preview and playback can expose
different defects.

## Learning that changes later work

Continuity is useful when retained knowledge improves what the assistant can
understand or do next. That knowledge may include why a message was formed,
which source is now current, what a person asked, who owns a follow-up, which
asset is selected and why an earlier approach needed correction.

Reflection can reveal that a fact changed, a method was insufficient or useful
knowledge existed but was never reached. Those differences call for different
responses. Update local state for the first; deepen or form a competence for
the second; repair the entry or composition for the third. A successful result
or a new possibility can also teach a method before anything fails.

The assistant carries the reusable reason into the competence or local
extension that should act differently. A source update makes that knowledge
available; later use can show whether it participated. Saving a preferred
sentence, asset or event record has a different function from improving the
method through which the next artifact is made.

Management, editorial work, design, domain understanding and relationships can
teach one another. Each retains its contribution: source facts stay with their
source, authorial meaning with its author, local policies with the user, and
execution state with the controller that can observe the effect. The
[evolution method](EVOLUTION.md) governs the return without requiring a new
manager or a permanent team of agents for every task.

## Product, user context and receiving environment

The public source owns the generalized method and its evolution. The user's
instance owns their project context, sources, preferences, relationships,
current work and local learning. The receiving AI environment provides the
interaction, persistence and tools through which those relations become
operative.

That separation allows an adopter to bring the method into an existing
configuration. A previously installed kernel may already provide continuity,
reflection or tool routing. Existing skills may carry domain or design
knowledge. Reconcile those contributions with Social Kernel, preserve their
valid methods and make the added knowledge reachable under the situation that
needs it. Do not infer replacement authority from the fact that an upstream
version is newer.

MAIOS Project Kernel or kernel_chat can supply useful capabilities when they
fit the user's environment. They remain optional contributors; Social Kernel
does not require a chain of installations or access to the builder's private
repositories.

The source has mechanical packaging profiles for OpenAI, Claude Code and
portable file use. A profile describes how source material is arranged. A
particular host's discovery, interpretation, persistence and tool execution
must be observed at that host. Follow
[host integration](docs/HOST_INTEGRATION.md),
[adoption](docs/ADOPTION.md) and the
[receiver contract](docs/RECEIVER_CONTRACT.md) for these practical relations.

## Autonomy through actual means

The host may expose generators, editors, browser or computer use, MCP tools,
connectors, callable API integrations or other execution routes. Choose a real
means for the selected operation and preserve its result. If one means is
unavailable, another suitable route can still make the work possible. When a
necessary operation cannot run, retain the source and prepared artifact and
leave a precise continuation for a capable receiver.

A valid mandate can cover repeated actions, including selection and execution
within its scope. The kernel should use that authority competently without
asking the user to decide every routine detail. Capability, account access and
a learned method do not widen the mandate. Publication, direct contact,
recurrence and spending remain actual effects with a known target, controller
and result.

Ongoing work also includes research, drafting, creative exploration, preparation
and review. These can proceed through their applicable authority even while
an independent platform operation remains unresolved. A wait belongs to the
condition or relationship that requires it.

## What the evidence can show

A generated file can establish that a production happened. Its inspection can
show specific properties under the observed conditions. A fictional exercise
can expose a method in use. A later task with changed circumstances can show
whether retained knowledge altered the work. Local source checks can establish
that the declared files, routes and package mechanics agree.

A real public encounter can produce attention, a question, criticism,
inspection, attempted use, adoption or a continuing relationship. Each
observation has its own meaning and coverage. Silence may leave the result
unknown; a reaction alone does not demonstrate adoption or establish what an
entire audience thinks.

The [current source state](CURRENT.md) and
[verification record](docs/VERIFICATION.md) distinguish these observations and
the versions to which they apply. Completing the source or assembling a new
package does not transfer a different receiver's proof to the new one.

## Evolving forms

Possible users include professionals, creators, researchers, maintainers,
companies and communities. Their purposes and useful operating forms differ.
The method can support an assistant working with the user, a private team
instance, recurring observation or bounded standing authority when actual
capability and policy make those forms useful.

Useful continuity should remain under the user's control. Readable sources,
recoverable files and a practical way to preserve or export local knowledge
support that direction. A service or business model should earn continued use
through accumulated usefulness.

The **0.2.0-dev.1 source candidate** develops the public method and its receiving
projections. Licensing and stable-release form remain owner decisions.
[Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) keeps further development and evidence needs reachable
without making them current delivered capability.
