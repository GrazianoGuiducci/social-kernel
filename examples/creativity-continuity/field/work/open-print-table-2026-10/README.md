## Selection and reasons

The operator's purpose is to help nearby adults discover an approachable way to make and share something. “Make a print. Take it home.” is the assistant's selected invitation for this event. It expresses the supported tangible outcome without inventing a technique, a booking process or a promised design.

The central image shows one simple print held by adult hands. The loose ink shapes, generous unprinted paper and visible irregularity make the depicted object modest and materially recognizable. The cream paper and deep blue type connect the notice to its printmaking subject; the restrained coral divider groups the participation details. These are situated choices for this event, not an approved logo or an enduring studio palette.

The title identifies the event; the short invitation gives a reason to join; the date and time remain prominent; the free drop-in and materials/experience details answer what joining involves. The caption adds “simple paper print” and repeats the practical information for an accessible standalone reading.
