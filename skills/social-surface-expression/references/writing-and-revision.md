# Conceive, write and revise social expression

Use this knowledge while a substantive artifact is being conceived, written or
revised. It deepens [Social Surface Expression](../SKILL.md). Reach the parts
that can change the work; a short reply may already contain everything needed.
An existing text, source, conversation or user observation can be enough to
begin. A complete campaign brief is not a prerequisite.

## Form what should become understandable

Recover the actual object, the author's direction and the source relation that
must survive expression. Understand the local encounter: what is already
visible, what question is open, and what this artifact could make possible.
That result may be recognition, understanding, a useful answer, a question or
an action. An explanation can finish with a new distinction; it does not need
an invitation to buy or follow.

Work backward from that relation to the premises a reader needs, then compose
them in an order that lets each support the next. For example, a claim about a
new service needs a recognizable service, what it actually changes and enough
mechanism to make that change intelligible. A feature list alone may leave its
purpose missing. An accurate example can reveal a mechanism but cannot choose
the author's subject in their place.

Keep the smallest useful writing note when the relation needs to survive
delegation or interruption: source and meaning to preserve, intended encounter,
necessary premises, voice, consequential claims and any available next action.
Use the user's existing work record rather than creating a second truth. A
reader model is provisional knowledge; observed questions can correct it.

Do not let the desired reaction supply a fact or force the argument. The
artifact makes relations available for the reader's own judgment. If the
source opens a question, preserve its openness; if the user has selected a
direction, continue it without turning every rewrite into another selection.

## Build the argument before polishing sentences

Let the form expose the source's real dependencies. A scene can make a problem
recognizable; an example can reveal a distinction; a mechanism can support a
consequence. Choose those that do necessary work. Simultaneous functions need
not become numbered steps, and a result need not become a chronological story.

An opening should make the subject, question, observation or consequential
difference present. A title identifies or opens that same object. A striking
hook that introduces a different promise cannot be repaired by a careful final
paragraph. Remove ceremonial introductions and claims about the importance of
the topic when the actual relation can begin the text directly.

Develop the necessary reasoning through evidence, explanation or an example
with a clear role. If the domain knowledge supplies only topic labels, return
to the pertinent source or competence; a polished outline cannot create the
missing understanding. An alternative, objection or limit enters when it
changes the claim or the reader's use. It need not be invented to make a text
look balanced.

End where the artifact has done its job. A reply may resolve the question. A
field note can leave a useful uncertainty visible. An instruction needs the
literal action and expected result. When inviting a next step, check that the
destination actually supports it. Do not repeat the introduction as a closing
summary when it adds no new relation.

## Give paragraphs and sentences a job

A paragraph should contribute one necessary movement and connect it to what
is already understood. Make its point specific, support it as needed and let
its consequence prepare what follows. These functions can fit in one sentence
or several; they are not four required slots.

When a longer draft feels repetitive or disconnected, name each paragraph's
job briefly. Two paragraphs doing the same job may need merging. A paragraph
with two indispensable jobs may need splitting. A section without a clear job
may belong in the deeper source rather than this artifact.

Choose precise subjects and verbs. Make a consequential abstraction visible
through what changes, where it changes and a useful example or mechanism.
Explain a specialized term before relying on it unless the local audience
already shares it. Pronouns, shorthand and compressed titles must still have
a recoverable referent in the actual visible context.

Let rhythm follow thought. A short sentence can hold a decisive distinction;
a longer one can preserve a dependency that separation would damage. Read key
passages as continuous prose to hear breathless accumulation, repeated cadence
or mechanical symmetry. Remove ornamental triplets, inert intensifiers,
generic claims of quality and transitions that announce a connection the text
never establishes. Retain an unusual phrase when it carries the source's
meaning rather than replacing it merely because a common phrase sounds smoother.

## Preserve the author's movement

Distinguish what the user supplied:

| Source relation | Writing treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Exact wording selected for use | Preserve it as instructed. Raise a consequential conflict before making a change that alters intent. |
| An observation, idea or authored explanation | Preserve its conceptual relation, emphasis and degree of determination; clean noise that carries no meaning. |
| A receiver's interpretation or proposed wording | Treat it as a candidate transformation, with material uncertainty visible. |

A rewrite can retain the facts while changing what gives rise to what, what
deserves attention, or how open the thought remains. Compare source and
candidate at those points. Source tension, ordering, lexical choice or cadence
may be part of the meaning. Fidelity does not require preserving accidental
transcription errors, and clarity does not license replacing an unfamiliar
idea with a familiar marketing category.

Past accepted language helps recognize an author's voice; it does not fix
their future register. A brief personal emphasis after an explained relation
may be appropriate in one artifact. It is not an instruction to insert an
aside into every post. When a fluent paragraph could describe many unrelated
objects after one noun is replaced, inspect what source-specific relation was
lost. Generic action labels can still be correct when the action is generic.

For person-to-person writing, keep internal analysis out of the greeting.
Start from something actually seen or known, the reason for writing and the
object being shared or answered. An inferred description of someone's motives
or strategy is not a fact about them. Read decisive lines as if spoken to that
person; retain technical depth where it helps the actual conversation.

## Give each surface its own encounter

A shared source can support several artifacts with different jobs:

| Form | Useful editorial relation |
| --- | --- |
| Reply | Reach the actual question and shared context; contribute the answer, distinction or evidence that helps there. |
| Standalone post or announcement | Make the subject recognizable and develop one useful relation without assuming earlier campaign knowledge. |
| Carousel or connected sequence | Give each part a contribution to the whole, with enough local context for the way it will be encountered. |
| Article, technical note or script | Supply the premises, support and depth its argument needs; let a spoken or timed form reshape syntax and pacing. |

These examples do not prescribe an artifact menu. Choose only forms that have
a reason. A short post is not necessarily an abbreviated article, and the
article need not exist first. A reader can enter through any available form.
Make links to deeper or adjacent work useful rather than requiring a fixed
path through the whole family.

When translating between languages, carry the same subject, claim state,
authorial direction and available action. Recompose idiom, sentence rhythm
and examples where the destination language needs it. Inspect each version;
approval of one language does not establish fidelity of another. A platform's
current limits or contribution rules should be checked when they affect the
selected artifact, instead of being inferred from a remembered character cap.

## Compose language and media together

Use [Social Media Composition](../../social-media-composition/SKILL.md) when
perception, image, sound, motion or interaction changes the result. This writing
method supplies authored meaning, argument, words, labels and narration. Media
composition supplies perceptual hierarchy, medium, visual or temporal form,
production and inspection. Each can change what the other needs to do.

Make the subject and dominant relation recoverable in the actual encounter.
The title, visible scene, image wording or an earlier slide can supply the
subject when the reader can really encounter it. A caption can deepen a visual
relation; it should not have to rescue a standalone image that points to no
recognizable object. Caption and visual must agree about capability, timing,
participants and the next action.

Let a layout constraint reveal whether a distinction needs a shorter sentence,
another hierarchy or a different medium. Do not delete the distinction merely
to fit the box. Conversely, an image that seems to imply a fact missing from
the source calls for source review or visual correction, not a new claim added
to the caption to make the pair appear consistent. A depiction generated for
illustration is not evidence that the pictured event occurred.

Preserve the shared meaning and relevant dependencies across selected forms.
A change in the source can affect a headline, voiceover and caption together;
a local spelling correction may affect only one variant. Agreement among
derivatives of one source does not independently confirm that source or show
that every derivative received the same correction.

## Review the artifact that will be encountered

Return to the source when a draft feels displaced. Locate the first loss:
wrong subject, attribution, selected example, missing premise, changed causal
order, wording or adaptation. Repair that relation before polishing the
nearest awkward sentence. Subtract repetition or an imported frame only when
its removal preserves the needed meaning.

Read the complete text in its selected form. Check the actual image, slide,
caption pair, transcript or other produced artifact with the available
inspection means. A source file or generation prompt cannot establish that
the rendered result is legible, that narration is intelligible or that a crop
preserves the necessary words. Use the media method for those checks. If only
text was inspected, say which part of the combined artifact remains unseen.

Review only the lenses that matter: fidelity and claim state; necessary
argument; specific support; source voice; understandable subject and action;
reading order and rhythm; truthful media/text relation; removal of semantic
repetition. For a consequential claim, retain the qualified source and its
meaningful scope. A number can remain the same while its metric, time span or
attribution changes; matching digits alone do not validate the statement.

Bind the useful review conclusion to the actual revision and assets inspected.
Record what was observed, what was corrected and any remaining limitation in
the work's existing continuity record. If a copy, source, crop or asset changes,
revisit the conclusions affected by it. Preserve an earlier successful check
at its original scope. A review of one variant is not approval of the latest
experiment, and editorial readiness does not authorize publication.

## Let the next artifact benefit

Keep the exact source relation, chosen copy and selected assets reachable with
their versions, reasons and current state. Follow the instance's existing
[state and records](../../../docs/STATE_AND_RECORDS.md) arrangements. File paths
must lead to material the next receiver can access; a lost temporary preview
does not provide continuity.

When a correction teaches something reusable, preserve the causal difference:
what the source meant, what the candidate added or removed, why that changed
the encounter and when the lesson applies. Return it through
[competence evolution](../../social-competence-evolution/SKILL.md) to the
writing method that should behave differently. If the knowledge already
existed, repair the entry that failed to reach it. A preferred sentence can
stay with its artifact; it need not become a general style rule.

At a later, different artifact, use the changed method and observe whether it
helps. Preserve the limits of that observation. A source author's review,
fictional exercise, real reader response and repeated use have different
evidential scope. Stop revising when another pass cannot change the selected
result materially.
