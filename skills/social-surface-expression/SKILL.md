# Social Surface Expression

Form the artifact from the actual source and user relation. This competence
owns conception, argument, language and revision of social expression. Its
[writing and revision method](references/writing-and-revision.md) supplies
portable editorial craft: use it when premise order, prose, voice or review
can change the artifact. An available editorial specialization can deepen this
knowledge through composition. Product truth, business decisions, design and
publication retain their own owners.

Use [opportunity formation](../social-opportunity-formation/SKILL.md) when the
movement is still being selected. The two competences can change one another's
understanding; do not finish the theme first and use writing only to polish it.

## Select the receiver's valuable possibility before phrasing

When the artifact is intended to help someone recognize a product, understand
a mechanism, ask a question, evaluate an offer or decide whether to continue,
its value is not automatically the last benefit the author described.
Compose the intended reader result with the **current capability and
relevant possibilities of the actual source system** and the receiving
situation *while the expression is being conceived*.
[Opportunity formation](../social-opportunity-formation/SKILL.md#form-source-bound-value-before-selecting-the-encounter)
owns the selection of the encounter; this competence owns which semantic
premises let its value become intelligible.

A compact internal relation is often sufficient:

```text
what should this reader be able to recognize or do?
+ what does the source system demonstrably do today?
+ what could it genuinely form or become through further situated work?
+ what does this reader actually know, face or have permission to do?
-> pertinent value difference, with its evidence state
-> minimum recognizable subject, premises, example and action
-> expression in this surface's own words/media
```

The value difference is a **possibility grounded in a relation**, not a
product slogan, a prediction of the reader's reaction or an asserted
need. Make the current ability legible at its proper strength; a
constructible extension is a proposal or hypothesis until actual work
exercises it. A source's technical mechanism can support very different
reader resultants without changing its identity or being copied unchanged
between surfaces. A question that reveals lack of access, proof, fit
or budget can legitimately change the proposed encounter; it is not
automatically resistance to defeat.

**Fictional contrast, not evidence of effectiveness:** suppose an open
project method already preserves corrections and reasons across two
work sessions, while integration with an external business system is only
proposed. A builder-facing explanation can show the current files and
how to inspect the method. A potential organizational partner may
benefit from a brief discussion of what a future, funded integration
would require, without promising it is installed. A research audience
might instead investigate how to verify that the retained correction
changes behavior on a non-identical later task. These are three
different useful results from a common source, not three repetitions
of one marketing call to action.

When the current source and recipient relation are already clear, choose
the smallest sufficient expression directly: this is acting knowledge,
not a mandatory multi-pass briefing or approval procedure. Let a real
reader question, response or failed encounter teach **which value and
premise were missing** and return that general writing distinction
to this competence. Source/product truth, private recipient context,
relationship state and effect authority remain with their actual
owners; a drafted message is not a sent or received one.

## Recover the source before the hook

Read the current source needed for the selected object. Distinguish exact user
wording, authored meaning, receiver interpretation, product or research fact,
external evidence and an open hypothesis. Preserve the attribution of each
where it changes meaning. A previous model choice must not silently acquire
the user's authority because it was recorded in a plan.

Identify the subject, the relation being expressed and the reason this
artifact is being formed. Understand what should become possible after the
encounter: recognition, a distinction, a useful answer, an informed question,
a contribution or another situated result. Regress to the minimum premises
that make that result understandable, then compose their order and wording.

Make the source relation available so the receiver can form its own judgment.
The intended result guides the artifact's semantic conditions; it is not a
predicted reaction or a requirement that another person agree. Preserve a
meaningful open alternative when the source has not determined it.

Do not infer the subject from the most convenient example, a popular phrase or
a generic benefit. Source truth can establish that an example is accurate
without establishing that it represents the user's intended object. If those
relations conflict, return to the last faithful source relation before writing
more copy.

## Short posts from a widely felt change — 8 November 2026

A short, dry social post ("post secco") can begin from an event that matters to people **outside the specialist community**: a profession changing, a task becoming automatable, an unfamiliar result, a decision that the reader can no longer interpret, or another actually recognizable change. Technical interest alone does not establish that common concern. When freshness matters, qualify the event with a current dated source; its publicity does not make a product claim true or guarantee reach.

Form the public encounter through the smallest causal difference: recognizable occurrence and human stake -> the question or tension it leaves for the reader -> a **currently available** competence or method that can change how this is handled -> a practical intervention or capacity. These are relations to compose, not a four-line template. A humorous inversion can carry the argument: a newly announced solution can itself create a problem of explanation and understanding. Preserve the real result's evidence state; a claimed proof and a universally accepted proof are different claims.

Make compactness semantic, not merely typographic. Keep the author's sharpness and odd but meaningful turn of phrase; repair pronoun agreement, grammatical subjects and conceptual overload without replacing the voice with a technology pitch. The first sentence should be recognizable without knowing the product. Name the existing competence at the point where it answers the **specific human difficulty**, with an inspectable public link when there is a usable resource. A method in development may already exist without having demonstrated universal effectiveness; the public wording should distinguish those states directly rather than through defensive disclaimers.

When a meme or short animation accompanies the post, compose with Social Media Composition: let the visual stage the same causal reversal (for example, *task completed -> new human question -> shared way to examine it*). Avoid making the visual a separate product advertisement or falsely depicting a demonstrated live product behavior. The caption retains the source and practical claim; the visual makes the core tension recognizable quickly. Re-read both together after generation.

## Preserve the two directions

In formation, the source precedes the public meaning. In the encounter, a
recognizable subject and intelligible relation may precede a technical term,
acronym or deeper citation. Arrange that encounter without removing the source
from the work that forms it.

Keep the subject recoverable from the artifact's visible context. A title,
image, preceding sentence or known discussion can carry it; private campaign
memory cannot. Introduce an acronym after the relation it compresses is
available, unless the actual audience already possesses it.

Preserve source-specific voice, tension and degree of determination when they
carry meaning. Translate what the surface requires; do not normalize unusual
but meaningful expression into a familiar institutional or marketing pattern.
A hypothesis can remain exploratory and an explicit determination can remain
direct. Evidence discipline does not require flattening both into cautious,
generic prose.

Treat learned voice and earlier accepted forms as situated knowledge. The
user can change register or direction; do not turn past wording into a frozen
profile or imitate punctuation in place of understanding its meaning.

## Make the form native to the encounter

Read enough of the actual discussion and surface context to understand its
question, shared premises, contribution norms and current constraints. A reply
should contribute to that relation. A standalone presentation should make its
object recognizable without assuming prior campaign knowledge.

The same source can produce different words, depth, order and actions across
surfaces. A brief signal can expose one relation; a technical contribution may
need code or inspectable evidence; a synthesis can connect several relations.
Choose the form for its job rather than translating a complete post unchanged
into every channel.

When image, diagram, sound, motion or interaction changes the encounter,
compose with [media composition](../social-media-composition/SKILL.md) during
formation. Let copy and perceptual form develop together.
Keep the subject and dominant relation understandable in the visual itself;
use the caption to deepen that relation. An inaccessible asset path does not
give the user an inspectable final artifact.

A visual result can expose that the wording implies a different object; an
indispensable verbal distinction can change the layout or medium. Return that
difference to their common source relation, then revise the affected parts.
Preserve meaning, source and reasons across a content family while each form
does its own job. A post need not wait for an unrelated video, and agreement
between generated derivatives does not supply independent evidence.

## Keep truth and public action aligned

Use the current truth owner for capability and availability. An observed fact,
working method, released capability, authorial direction, research hypothesis,
external claim and inference carry different commitments. Retain the needed
qualification without building the text around imagined objections.

If a current observation conflicts with the maintained source, preserve the
discrepancy and qualify the contested claim while it is resolved. Source
ownership does not make a record infallible. Return a material correction to
that owner; a third-party label alone does not redefine the source object.

Make a material evidence route reachable where it helps inspection or use.
External research can describe a condition without validating this product.
A listing can show discoverability without proving use or endorsement.

If the artifact invites an action, make that action available at the actual
destination. Reading source, requesting a demonstration, installing a release
and authorizing an operation are different actions. Explain necessary
conditions at the point where they change what the reader can do.

Before any private context enters an artifact or external tool request, use
[user-field projection](../social-user-field-projection/SKILL.md).

## Revise the relation, then the sentences

Use the [local craft reference](references/writing-and-revision.md) to revise
argument, paragraphs, sentence rhythm and situated voice where they matter.
Its lenses are available knowledge, not a fixed number of review passes.

Read the result as an encounter whose reader lacks the hidden authoring
context. Check whether the subject remains clear, each necessary premise is
available, and the language preserves what the source actually says. Remove a
sentence that adds a foreign category or defensive frame without contributing
to the selected meaning.

When a mismatch appears, trace its first loss: source attribution, topic
selection, example, premise order, wording or surface adaptation. Editing the
nearest awkward phrase will not correct an earlier wrong subject.

Preserve the exact draft revision and any final asset needed for review or
execution. Review the actual composed artifact: inspect the rendered media
with its copy, reading order and destination when those determine the
encounter. State a review's unobserved part if the necessary preview is
unavailable. A changed copy, crop, asset or source invalidates only the review
conclusions that depend on that change.

Use [effect boundary](../social-effect-boundary/SKILL.md) for the
separate publication action. Respect the actual current mandate; editorial
coherence, a topic approval and a published-effect receipt are different
things.

A correction that changes later expression belongs in the nearest writing
method. If the knowledge was available but failed to shape the artifact,
repair selection or routing. An isolated preferred sentence can stay with its
artifact. A recipient's reaction is evidence about the encounter, not
authority to redefine the source or the user's direction.
