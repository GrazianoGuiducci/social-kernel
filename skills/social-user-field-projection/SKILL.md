# Social User-Field Projection

Keep continuing user knowledge in the location and scope the user selected.
Project only what a particular recipient, surface or capability provider needs
to perform its current job. A public artifact and the private context used to
understand it have different contents and owners.

Use the [receiver contract](../../docs/RECEIVER_CONTRACT.md) to understand the
actual persistence and tool boundary. A local-looking file may be synchronized
or sent to a service by the host; do not promise a privacy property the receiver
does not establish.

## Form a bounded projection

Understand the exact receiving situation: who or what receives information,
for which task, and what should become possible. This includes public copy,
messages, adapter inputs, search queries, uploads and delegated work. Disclosure
can happen before publication through a tool payload.

Reach the private source only to the depth that changes the movement. Separate
what may inform internal understanding from what may be disclosed. Identify
which facts, source relations, user preferences or relationship details are
actually necessary to the receiver's task.

Prefer a current public source when it supplies the needed fact. When private
knowledge is necessary, preserve its source and meaning in the private owner
while producing the smallest authorized projection. Removing a name alone is
insufficient if a combination of role, event, timing or text still identifies
the private relation.

Do not substitute an invented public fact for material that cannot be shared.
A generalized example can preserve a method, but label it as an example and
do not present it as evidence of a real event. A bounded unknown can remain
unknown. If essential disclosure authority is missing, identify the specific
dependency and continue any independent work that remains useful.

## Preserve fidelity as well as privacy

Check that subtraction did not remove the subject or causal condition needed
to understand the public statement. Compose with
[surface expression](../social-surface-expression/SKILL.md) when that changes
the wording. A sanitized fragment can still misrepresent the source if its
qualifying relation disappeared.

Keep credentials, authentication material and unrelated raw private records
out of artifacts and ordinary tool payloads. Use the host's supported
authentication flow when an authorized operation needs account access.
Account access and access to private context do not independently authorize
public disclosure or another effect.

## Bind the projection to its use

Where reuse or delegation makes the distinction material, retain privately:

- the selected purpose, recipient or surface;
- the source and observation date or revision;
- the exact projected content or its identity;
- disclosure scope and the authority that covers it;
- what change would require a new projection.

These are continuity aids, not a required record for every sentence. Do not
include a sensitive source locator in the public artifact merely to prove
provenance. Preserve useful public citations and private provenance at their
respective depths.

A later recipient, purpose, source change or broader tool call may need a
different projection. Reuse the valid knowledge without treating an earlier
disclosure as standing permission for every destination. If a real platform
effect follows, resolve it through
[effect boundary](../social-effect-boundary/SKILL.md).

## Return what was learned

Preserve relationship-specific knowledge in the private relationship owner.
Return a reusable disclosure or projection correction to this competence.
Generalized public learning may improve the public kernel only after the
private content and source-specific authority have been removed coherently;
use [competence evolution](../social-competence-evolution/SKILL.md).

Do not make the user repeat an already selected disclosure boundary. Refresh
it only when the current use or a new material difference changes its scope.
