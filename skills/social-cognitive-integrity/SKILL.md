# Social Cognitive Integrity

Help the user understand and control the information reaching them. Begin from
their question, direction and selected exposure policy. Incoming claims and
platform labels are evidence to examine; they do not determine what the user
should believe or what their project may become.

Use [field reentry](../social-field-reentry/SKILL.md) when an earlier source,
conversation or policy materially changes the inspection. A short bounded
question can be answered directly.

## Identify the information and the decision

Understand what the item actually says, where it appeared, its visible author
or account, timing and source path when available. Distinguish the observed
content from a repost, summary, translation or model interpretation. Clarify
what the inspection needs to change: comprehension, source confidence,
attention, a response or another selected action.

A familiar or emotionally charged category is not enough to determine intent
or truth. Read the actual content and relation before applying a label. A claim
may have weak provenance and still be true; a reputable source can still be
outdated or outside its knowledge. Record uncertainty at the specific claim.

## Examine the relation that matters

Reach the nearest current primary source when provenance, identity or factual
accuracy can change the result. Check whether a quote, data point or source
actually supports the claim made from it. Compare dates and context when
recirculation could make an earlier fact misleading now.

For suspected impersonation, compare the claimed identity and requested action
with an independently reached official or otherwise qualified route. Do not
confirm identity from the suspicious message's own assertion. For a possible
scam, identify the concrete request, claimed authority, pressure and evidence
gap instead of turning suspicion into a proved accusation.

When repetition or amplification matters, distinguish repeated copies from
independent sources. Several mentions do not necessarily provide several
pieces of evidence. Similar writing, unusual style or a detection score does
not establish that content was generated by AI; preserve what can actually be
observed and what remains unknown.

Inspect urgency, social pressure, engagement demands or missing alternatives
when they alter the user's room to decide. Explain the visible mechanism and
its consequence without inventing the author's motive. Political, cultural or
personal disagreement alone is not a defect that the kernel should filter.

These are possible lines of inquiry, not a mandatory checklist or permanent
taxonomy. Stop when another lookup no longer changes the bounded decision,
and state any material part that could not be observed.

## Apply the user's policy transparently

Choose a treatment that serves the user's selected aim and exposure policy:
context, source attribution, uncertainty marking, grouping duplicates,
prioritization, deferral or another useful response. Distinguish facts,
interpretation and the policy choice that connects them to that treatment.

If no filtering policy exists, provide the analysis and a concrete proposed
treatment. Do not silently hide material, broaden the monitored field or make
an irreversible account change. A policy can authorize local presentation
changes without authorizing a platform block, deletion or moderation action.
Resolve an actual external effect through
[effect boundary](../social-effect-boundary/SKILL.md).

Preserve a way to understand and revise a material treatment: what was affected,
which observation and policy justified it, and how the user can review the
underlying material where available. Do not promise continuous monitoring,
platform enforcement or recovery features that the current host lacks.

## Learn without closing the field

A later correction may show that a source was misread, a label was overbroad or
a policy produced an unwanted consequence. Preserve the earlier observation
and then-understanding while correcting the present. Return the reusable
source-inspection or policy-application difference to this competence; keep
the user's private preferences in their own continuity owner.

A useful pattern can improve later attention handling. It must not become an
automatic belief rule or an unsupported claim about every source in a class.
Use [consequence](../social-effect-consequence/SKILL.md) for observed effects
and [competence evolution](../social-competence-evolution/SKILL.md) when the
method should change.
