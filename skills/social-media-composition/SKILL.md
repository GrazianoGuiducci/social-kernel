# Social Media Composition

Make the relevant subject, relation or change perceptible through a usable
artifact. This competence carries the public design method needed for bounded
social work: choosing a medium, composing its hierarchy, producing the media,
reading what was actually produced and preserving the result for later use.

Use [surface expression](../social-surface-expression/SKILL.md) as consequential
words, labels, captions or narration form. Language can change the composition;
an image, source asset or spatial constraint can change the question and wording.
Product and research sources retain their facts, the user retains direction,
and [effect boundary](../social-effect-boundary/SKILL.md) governs publication.
The competent roles can compose inside one receiver.

## Find what should become perceptible

Read the selected source and the current receiving situation. Establish the
subject, the relation worth making visible, what the person already recognizes,
and the understanding or action this encounter should make possible. A vivid
image, a clear diagram and a useful photograph can perform different jobs.
Choose from the work, without assuming every post needs an image or every
complex subject needs an infographic.

Let deeper source understanding shape a simpler expression. Preserve the
subject, dominant relation and any boundary that changes its meaning; retain
unused depth in the source or companion artifact. Surface conventions such as
short copy, bright color or a call to action are options whose usefulness
depends on this encounter.

A newly available resource may improve the movement itself. A photograph can
reveal an activity worth explaining; a rendered draft can expose a relationship
that the initial words missed. Revisit the source and co-form the better
expression with the writing method. An attractive generated connection remains
a proposal until its meaning is supported; it cannot create product facts.

Recover the source and selected state of an existing asset before editing it.
Inspect the actual image, slide or sequence, including labels, dates, marks,
captions and destinations that can change attribution. The asset records an
earlier transformation. Current evidence or intent may require a targeted
correction while the rest remains useful.

## Form the representation field before choosing the medium

When the work starts from a concept, new learning or an emerging relation,
do not begin by asking which familiar format to produce. First understand
**where the concept becomes real and what changes there**.

A useful formation relation is:

```text
source-bound concept
+ domain or real receiving field
+ concrete use case
+ cognitive / operational dynamic
+ practical consequence
+ receiving situation
-> representation field
-> medium and artifact
```

These are coordinates for understanding, not a mandatory form.

**Domain / receiving field** asks where the concept is actually encountered:
a team, professional practice, research group, organization, software project,
community, public presence or another situated field.

**Use case** asks what someone is doing: correcting an interpretation, teaching
a method, delegating work, comparing evidence, returning after interruption,
changing tools, coordinating several contributors or another real movement.

**Dynamic** asks what produces the difference: a source becoming pertinent,
a correction changing the method, experience becoming reusable knowledge,
several competences composing, a consequence altering later work, or another
relation supported by the source.

**Practical consequence** asks what is different afterward. It may be less
reconstruction, a reusable method, clearer responsibility, a different next
decision, more selective supervision or another supported result. Do not turn
a plausible consequence into measured performance without evidence.

The representation should let a receiver perceive that relation before
internal vocabulary is required. A new concept may therefore need a different
visual grammar from the last successful post.

### Reusable representation possibilities

The following are learned possibilities, not templates or a required taxonomy:

- **temporal phases** when roles or participation visibly change over time;
- **causal / expanding spiral** when a consequence returns and changes later work;
- **non-identical before / after** when learning is shown by a later different case;
- **operating field** when several simultaneous actors, sources or constraints matter;
- **supervision gradient** when human involvement changes continuously;
- **possibility -> resultant -> next field** when open alternatives contract into
  a determination that changes what follows;
- **surface / depth** when visible work depends on deeper reasons, sources or methods;
- **one work object / multiple receivers** when tools or agents change around a
  continuing project;
- **present -> direction -> horizon** when current capability and future possibility
  must remain distinct;
- **real-case demonstration** when a concrete movement explains the concept better
  than an abstract diagram;
- **3D, motion or interaction** when viewpoint, transformation or exploration
  contributes meaning that a simpler static form would lose.

If none of these preserve the source relation, form another representation.
A recurring new grammar can become reusable learning through
[competence evolution](../social-competence-evolution/SKILL.md).

### Correct the representation while it forms

A representation itself can distort the object. A central node can invent
authority; a loop can imply repetition; an unattended agent can imply
unbounded autonomy; a shield can reduce a source/coherence relation to generic
security; a line can imply automatic synchronization.

When a visual form changes the source, object, status or possibility, correct
the representation before polishing it.

Changing representation can also expose a hidden relation:

```text
same source/object + representation A -> perceived relation A
same source/object + representation B -> perceived relation B

difference
-> source-grounded relation
 | representation artifact
 | no_change
```

The second representation is not automatically more true. Return the
difference to the source and current movement.

### Mother visual and media family

When one concept supports several public forms, a **mother visual** or media
nucleus can preserve the central relation while derivatives change density,
sequence and carrier.

Possible derivatives include a social image, pill, carousel, presentation,
short video, longer explainer or interactive surface. Produce only the forms
that serve a real receiving situation. Derivatives preserve the source relation,
not merely the crop or style of the first artifact.

## Transmute internal knowledge into a public possibility

Internal taxonomies, system labels and implementation vocabulary can guide the
work without becoming the public language of the artifact.

When the audience does not need the technical mechanism itself, transform the
underlying relation into a scene, role or situation in which a person can
recognize a possible way of working.

```text
internal/source depth
-> understand the dynamic
-> show a recognizable situation
-> show what becomes different
-> keep further possibility open
```

A useful media artifact can shape expectation. If the system genuinely supports
more than the receiver currently imagines, a source-grounded visual can help
them see what could be attempted. That changed expectation can influence how
they use, teach, delegate to or explore the system.

Do not confuse this with promising an outcome. Preserve the distinction between
current capability, a reachable direction and an open horizon. A desirable
future relation may be shown as possibility, not as an already observed fact.

Prefer media in which the receiver can think:

```text
I can see myself using this;
I understand what changes in the work;
I can imagine further possibilities beyond this one example.
```

Avoid turning one platform, workflow, interface or current tool into the whole
identity of the system.

## Choose and compose the medium

Select the carrier through which the relation becomes understandable:

| Perceptual job | Useful form when the context supports it |
| --- | --- |
| Recognize a subject, material or situated activity | An owned photograph, a relevant existing image or a generated illustration. |
| Retain one distinction or see a focused transformation | A composed image, typographic treatment or short sequence. |
| Compare quantities or understand typed relations together | A chart, diagram, table or spatial composition built from the actual evidence. |
| See what changes over time | A storyboard followed by an executed sequence, animation or video when those means are available. |
| Explore how a choice changes the result | An interactive artifact when its behavior contributes to understanding. |

These are possible forms. Their presence does not require a campaign family
or a particular renderer. Preserve any useful existing medium when it still
performs the job.

Choose a dominant object and a readable path through the composition. Use
scale, interval, grouping, alignment, typography, value and color according to
their roles. A strong focal event needs enough quiet support to remain clear.
A source-specific visual relation should still make sense when decorative
polish is removed. Negative space can separate and pace; filling every area
usually creates competing messages.

The visible subject and material relation should be recoverable at the intended
encounter. Companion text can deepen, qualify or open another relation. When a
necessary premise does not fit, change the arrangement or divide the work across
meaningful frames before shrinking essential text into decoration.

Read the user's current brand or project profile and the relevant selected
references. Preserve supported identity, approved marks and stable visual roles.
Treat experiments and older preferences according to their actual status.
Without a profile, form a situated candidate from the subject and record the
useful reasons. The method's origin supplies no default palette or brand.

## Keep the form faithful to its source

A line, arrow, grouping, scale or animated transition makes a claim. Preserve
the actual relation: sequence, containment, comparison, access and causation
need distinguishable expression. Similar placement cannot establish equivalent
roles, and a visual link cannot invent an integration or automatic operation.

Keep external evidence, interpretation, current capability and future direction
recognizable where they carry different commitments. Localize volatile figures,
dates, availability and destinations so later corrections reach their dependent
artifacts. A strong presentation of a possibility preserves its status.

In a sequence, make the meaningful change visible in the scene or relationship.
Changing only a caption may leave the represented event unchanged. Give the
viewer time to recognize the relevant state, preserve orientation between
frames, and use motion when it explains something time adds. Identify an
illustration or reconstruction when presenting it as observation would change
the meaning.

When exact wording, data, URLs or geometry matter, use a controllable text,
vector, chart or layout layer suited to the real tools. Generated imagery can
supply material, scene, metaphor or atmosphere around that layer. If a raster
tool must carry exact content, verify every consequential element in its actual
output before selecting it.

## Produce with the receiver's real means

Read [production and continuity](references/production-and-continuity.md) when
executing, editing, inspecting or handing off media. It carries the practical
method for matching tools, making a useful production brief, reviewing output
and preserving versions.

Use callable means available in the current environment and follow their
actual instructions. A renderer name, API key or completed prompt does not
establish a working execution path. When the selected operation is possible
and authorized, produce the artifact. When one operation is unavailable,
preserve the formed work, consider an available equivalent that keeps the
relation, and identify the exact receiving task that remains.

A production brief gives the tool the source-bound perceptual thesis,
composition, concrete references, selected invariants, exact-content needs and
expressive freedom. It is an execution input; the source and design reasons
remain reachable outside it.

For an edit, use the actual selected artifact as the reference and state the
observed change required. Preserve its useful identity, layout and source
relationship unless the selected work calls for a new direction. Inspect the
new result before making another correction; neither a fixed attempt count
nor indefinite aesthetic variation is a quality method.

## Read the artifact as an encounter

Open or render the output through the means actually available. Inspect the
final crop, dimensions and likely viewing scale, plus playback when time
carries meaning. A desktop master and a plausible feed preview expose different
issues. Distinguish a simulated preview from a readback in the actual platform.

Read both the perceptual event and the consequential details: is the subject
clear, does the hierarchy carry the intended relation, do words remain legible,
do image and text agree, and has the rendering added an unsupported connection,
state or attribution? Check the actual asset rather than treating a tool's
success response as this observation.

Provide an accessible reading appropriate to the selected artifact: accurate
alternative text, a caption or transcript, a linear explanation for spatial
meaning, or an equivalent for motion when needed. Preserve the useful
information instead of describing only visual decoration.

Correct the strongest observed mismatch through its cause. The source may need
qualification, the wording may need recomposition, or the visual may need a
targeted edit. Preserve good parts and stop when another change adds no
material improvement. An uninspected artifact can be handed off with its
inspection gap; its quality remains unverified.

## Preserve the result and let later work begin differently

Keep the actual selected file and its editable source when available, the
relevant source/profile, copy revision, production instructions, inspection
and selection reason in the user's chosen continuity. Use identities and paths
that the next receiver can really reach. A delivered preview, a source file
and the editable master may be different objects.

Preserve earlier candidates when their differences explain the decision.
Latest generated, selected for this use, approved for an exact effect and
published remain distinct states. A positive response to one visual feature
does not select every experimental variant or authorize a social effect.

Before a later derivative, recover that selection and reread the changed
situation. Carry the source relation into the new format, audience, language
or availability; recompose layout and emphasis where necessary. Update the
dependent copy, labels and media together while preserving valid unrelated
work.

A reusable perceptual or production lesson belongs in this method or a local
design extension that later work will reach. Project-specific identity belongs
in the profile; words and argument return to the writing method; a broken
tool route belongs to its receiver. Use
[competence evolution](../social-competence-evolution/SKILL.md) when the learning
should alter a continuing method. A single artifact preference can stay with
that artifact.
