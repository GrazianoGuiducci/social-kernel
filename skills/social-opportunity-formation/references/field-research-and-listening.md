# Field Research and Listening

Acquire the knowledge that can change a social movement. Listening can uncover
a question, useful possibility, relationship or change in meaning. It need not
end in a post, a report or an expanded monitoring system.

## Form an observation with a purpose

Begin from the current direction and what is missing from the understanding.
What might a new observation change: the topic, reader's situation, source
claim, place of encounter, form, timing or next relationship step? If the user
has selected exploration, inspect enough of the field to let a useful question
emerge rather than inventing a campaign to organize it.

Possible entries include an unanswered question in an existing conversation,
several people describing a related difficulty, a source development, an
independent mention, or new work adjacent to a capability the user can offer.
A known source or useful idea can also initiate the encounter before public
attention gathers around it.

Choose the observation route for the relation that matters. The original
conversation owns what someone asked; a project source owns its described
capability; a current source can establish availability; a captured page can
establish what was visible at that moment. No source type establishes every
kind of fact. A summary or search result can help reach the decisive source.

## Understand the actual encounter

Read enough of a conversation to understand the question, preceding answers,
current request and what a contribution could add. A saved prepared argument
may be irrelevant to the present exchange. A person may need an example,
an inspectable object, an explanation, a direct answer or a better question.
Let their work change the evidence and form before selecting the contribution.

When exploring a wider field, inspect only the sources that can expose a new
relation. Existing sources and relationships remain useful starting points;
follow an adjacent person, project or discussion when that edge changes the
decision. Stop expanding when it adds only another instance of what is already
sufficiently understood.

Distinguish observed content from attributed report, inference and unknown.
Read the source behind a consequential claim when the difference could change
the movement. Compare dates or versions where recirculation may have altered
meaning. Source evidence can correct the user's or receiver's understanding
without silently selecting a different purpose for them.

For specific uncertainty, provenance or pressure, use
[cognitive integrity](../../social-cognitive-integrity/SKILL.md). A surprising
idea or unfamiliar viewpoint is not itself a reason to exclude the source.

## Adapt discovery to the real means

One tool may find topics, another may resolve a known profile, and a third may
read a thread. If a connector cannot discover by subject, another available
authorized source can reveal relevant projects or people; the connector may
then resolve their identity. Preserve the discovery source and verify the
current relation before joining facts or preparing person-directed work.

An unavailable connector result does not prove that a person or conversation
is absent. Ambiguous identity remains ambiguous. Do not substitute another
person because their profile was easier to retrieve. Use the
[receiver contract](../../../docs/RECEIVER_CONTRACT.md) when a real capability
or bounded handoff must be established. Reach only public or authorized source
material and project the minimum private context needed into an external query
through [user-field projection](../../social-user-field-projection/SKILL.md).

## Keep attention and evidence meaningful

Preserve the observation time and coverage when they affect interpretation.
One visible feed is one observation window. Several copies of a claim need not
be independent evidence; recurring questions may share an original source.
An apparent absence is limited by what could be inspected.

Attention, truth, fit, demand and useful consequence answer different
questions. A much-discussed subject may have no useful relation to the selected
work. A quiet technical exchange may expose an important new possibility.
Do not manufacture a global trend ranking from a small sample or discard a
legitimate encounter because its result cannot be predicted.

## Return the useful difference

Make the smallest result that lets
[opportunity formation](../SKILL.md) continue: the question or possibility,
material evidence and source, interpretation or uncertainty, why it matters,
and what useful movement follows. A clear source note may suffice.

Preserve a continuing observation only when its source identity, watched
relation and next meaningful trigger can help later work. The trigger may be
a reply, changed availability, a promised artifact or a real timing condition.
Selected recurring work uses [recurrent operation](../../../docs/RECURRENT_OPERATION.md)
through the means the receiver actually provides. One useful lookup does not
create an ongoing monitor.

When a source correction or later consequence changes how listening should
work, update this method or its entry. A better search route, a repeatedly
misread question or a distinction between independent and repeated signals can
change future observation. Keep relationship facts in their own continuity;
send reusable expression, media or source learning to the owner that should
act differently. Do not generalize one local observation into a universal
belief or audience rule.
