# Social Relationship Continuity

Recover the person or group and the relation that matters now. An account can
carry several relationships; matching a name or accepting an invitation does
not settle their meaning.

## Recover the actual relation

Use attributed interaction and current evidence to distinguish the counterpart,
thread, purpose, last material event, present state and next trigger owner.
Inspect further personal history only when it changes this interaction.

Before a person-directed effect, resolve the correct recipient/account from the
actual thread and current evidence. Do not choose among ambiguous people from
a familiar name alone.

Keep useful distinctions separate:

- unanswered invitation, accepted connection and substantive conversation;
- polite acknowledgement and interest in a concrete next step;
- paid placement offer and independent editorial/testing interest;
- technical feedback, product use and commercial commitment;
- no reply, explicit defer, counterparty-owned reactivation and closure.

An event can alter several relations without making them interchangeable.

## Form a useful encounter

For a new or renewed relationship, understand the person's current work and
the object that makes an encounter useful now. Recover what each side could
contribute and the smallest question, source or artifact that makes that
relation concrete. Use [field research and listening](../social-opportunity-formation/references/field-research-and-listening.md)
when the relevant work or source is not yet understood.

Let the actual relation select the approach. Independent review, peer
discussion, creative collaboration, contribution and a commercial inquiry can
involve the same person while needing different objects and next steps.
Audience size, a role label or a connection alone does not establish fit.
An encounter can still be useful while its eventual consequence is unknown;
do not require a predicted positive response to preserve a coherent possibility.

Make the invitation or answer source-grounded and useful to this person.
Use [surface expression](../social-surface-expression/SKILL.md) while the
message forms. If a distinct business, domain or design function becomes
material, compose its available knowledge without turning that owner into the
controller of every relationship effect.

## Let the next trigger determine follow-up

If the counterpart will return when a condition becomes relevant, record that
the next trigger belongs to them. Do not schedule pressure merely because time
passes or a record contains a contact.

A later material event can reopen a deferred relation. Inspect that event and
purpose rather than treating waiting or closure as universal permanent rules.

If an unresolved question or agreed commitment belongs to us, prepare and
complete the useful next step through its authority. Already covered ordinary
follow-up does not need the same approval again.

A budget question, invitation or connection does not authorize accepting terms,
promising outcomes or sending private messages beyond the granted scope.

## Preserve what changes later work

Store minimum identity, source/thread, change, reason, next trigger and owner
in the private instance. Keep occurrence and later interpretation separate.
Use [state contracts](../../docs/STATE_AND_RECORDS.md) when needed.

Retain contact-discovery provenance when it matters. Missing provenance stays
missing; do not construct a convenient source after a challenge.

Return business/value learning to the appropriate owner; expression learning
to the editorial owner; cross-event social continuity to this competence.
Use [effect boundary](../social-effect-boundary/SKILL.md) before a message,
invitation or mutation. Record occurrence separately from delivery, acceptance,
usefulness and the next consequence.
