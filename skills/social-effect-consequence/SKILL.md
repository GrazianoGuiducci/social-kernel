# Social Effect and Consequence

Recover event identity, then inspect the later difference that can change work.

## Qualify the observation

Distinguish event, observer, observation time, coverage and native source.
Reading a record of an earlier platform readback is not a new platform observation.

Use distinctions when they matter: visibility, recognition, inspection,
conversation, correction, relationship, reported testing, observed use,
adoption, contribution, moderation, independent propagation, commercial signal,
no_change or unknown.

A third party saying they tested something is an attributed report. A directory
listing establishes that the directory represents the object; its badge is that
directory's classification. Neither proves that a receiver encountered or used
the product, or establishes product validity or endorsement. Do not repair a
product solely to satisfy an unexplained external marker.

Preserve partial coverage. No response seen in one view does not establish no
response everywhere. Temporal succession alone does not establish causality.

## Preserve time while changing understanding

Keep these relations recoverable:

1. What occurred and what evidence supports it.
2. What was understood or available then.
3. What later observation made a new relation visible.
4. What is understood now and which action or method should change.

For example, a fictional service announcement described a feature correctly
at publication. A later withdrawal changes queued copies and current reader
guidance. Retain the original event and source; requalify dependent copies
without claiming the earlier author knew the future.

An earlier source/intent substitution is different: recover the last faithful
relation and identify what the receiver introduced. In both cases preserve
occurrence instead of rewriting history.

## Return to the owner that should act differently

Ask what this makes possible or necessary next. A consequence may change the
question monitored, artifact, surface, relationship, source truth, competence
selection or handoff between owners.

Route distinct differences to responsible owners:

- relationship or next trigger -> [relationship continuity](../social-relationship-continuity/SKILL.md);
- expression or reader misunderstanding -> [surface expression](../social-surface-expression/SKILL.md);
- opportunity or encounter method -> [opportunity formation](../social-opportunity-formation/SKILL.md);
- source fact -> the corresponding product/research/source owner;
- reusable know-how or discovery -> [competence evolution](../social-competence-evolution/SKILL.md).

Several returns do not merge authority. Preserve an interface failure when
translation, assumed knowledge or the handoff itself caused the loss.

A receipt or metric can remain local state without becoming a general lesson.
For reusable learning, change the acting method or entry before the next
dependent movement. Later non-identical use can show whether that change helped.

## Continue from the resulting field

Preserve the current relation, reason, uncertainty and next trigger in the
instance; keep exact receipts and method depth reachable. See
[state and records](../../docs/STATE_AND_RECORDS.md).

With weak consequence, consider a meaningful change in entry, carrier, format,
audience, timing or evidence. Waiting remains useful while its reason applies;
it is not a requirement to disappear until every earlier event resolves.
