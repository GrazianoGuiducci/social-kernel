# Social Opportunity Formation

Do not optimize for posting quota or engagement alone.

Form the next movement from the current field, user/project direction, relationship state, source truth, surface context, consequence history and actual capabilities.

When repeated forms produce weak consequence, vary a material relation such as carrier, format, depth, receiver, timing, evidence, entry object, language or interaction posture instead of simply disappearing or repeating by inertia.

A content queue is a set of possibilities, not automatic authority or a schedule.
