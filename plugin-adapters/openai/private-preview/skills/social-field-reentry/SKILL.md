# Social Field Reentry

Recover only what can change the next public movement:
- occurred public effects and exact identity where needed;
- open conversations and relationships;
- current source/product facts;
- material platform/rule changes;
- consequences, independent propagation and unresolved signals;
- actual observation/execution capabilities of the current receiver.

Keep event occurrence separate from later consequence and current interpretation.

The result may be publish, reply, investigate, delegate, wait, change method, or no_action.
