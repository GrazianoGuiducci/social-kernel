work and receiver experience. The knowledge an adopter needs lives in the
[native competences](../COMPETENCES.md), the [kernel](../KERNEL.md) and their
linked guides. The sources below preserve provenance and offer further depth;
they are not an installation chain.

A 5 October 2026 private editorial/design movement also exposed a reusable
source-neutral relation now carried directly by the public media method:
**form the representation field before choosing the familiar format**.

```text
concept
+ domain / concrete use case
+ cognitive or operational dynamic
+ practical consequence
+ receiving situation
-> representation field
-> selected medium
```

The public method does not depend on that private repository, campaign, brand or
conversation. Its transferable relation is fully stated in
[Social Media Composition](../skills/social-media-composition/SKILL.md) and
[Recurrent Operation](RECURRENT_OPERATION.md). Private examples, target maps and
campaign decisions remain private.

Construction also drew on private editorial, social, business and
metacompetence practice. Its transferable decisions, reasons and conditions
are expressed in the public methods. Private campaigns, accounts, contacts,
conversation history and source topology remain with their owners. The
adopter supplies their own purpose, sources, context and actual means.

## Keep derivation and receiver evidence distinct

A source supports the relation attributed to it. A public method makes that
knowledge available in this product. A delivered bundle can make its body and
references reachable. Actual receiver work shows which of those relations
participated, through which means and with what result.

For example, the design sources explain how perceptual form can preserve or
alter meaning. A generated artifact and its inspection can show a particular
exercise of the transferred method. Neither the source link nor that one
artifact establishes compatibility with another program, independent
adoption or social effectiveness.

Use [Verification](VERIFICATION.md) for the observed receiver, source identity,
tools, supplied context, additional help and actual scope of each exercise.
Retain source representation, reachability, discovery, exercise and later
non-identical use as distinct evidence. A conceptual source and a local
example cannot supply unobserved public consequences.

## Qualify what can cross from private work

Recover the actual occurrence, what was understood then, the reusable
difference now visible, its evidence and the owner whose behavior should
change. Separate a user-specific preference or authority from the method's
more general condition. If removal of identifying details removes the
condition that makes the method work, develop a useful generalized relation
before transferring it.

Keep private records, identities, account state, unpublished strategy and
source topology with their owners. Explicitly fictional examples can explain
a mechanism and support a bounded exercise. They do not become anonymized
proof of real public success.

For learning native to Social Kernel, this repository owns the public
generalized method. A distinct editorial, visual, business or research lesson
returns to the owner whose later work must change. Shared source meaning
remains reachable while each owner carries its own practical consequence.

## Make the public result useful on its own

State what the receiver can now understand or do, where the knowledge acts and
what changes in another situation. Supply the operating relation, relevant
conditions and observable limits. Essential know-how belongs in the public
body even when the builder used deeper sources to form it.

If the receiver lacks a required source or callable means, preserve that exact
gap and the useful work already possible. Keep a proposed adaptation, performed
generation, inspected artifact and learned method at their actual state. A
qualified local exercise can be useful before broader evidence exists.

## Return what public use teaches

A user's context can preserve, alter or reject the transferred relation.
Recover the actual difference and return it to the public method, the private
source or another owner where it changes later behavior and the write is
selected. Preserve both useful contributions when receiving a later update.

Public and private knowledge can evolve through this exchange. A new source
version does not itself replace a user's learning, and a local correction
does not itself update an installed receiver or publish a contribution.
