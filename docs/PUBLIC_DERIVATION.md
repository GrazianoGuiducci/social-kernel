# Derive a public method from private learning

Private work can expose a method useful to another person without making the private record a public artifact. Transfer the relation that changes later work, with enough context to remain useful.

## Qualify what can cross

Recover the actual occurrence, what was understood then, the reusable difference now visible, its evidence and the owner whose behavior should change. Separate user-specific preference or authority from the method's more general condition. If the condition disappears when identifying details are removed, the candidate needs further formation rather than a stronger claim.

Keep private records, identities, account state, unpublished strategy and source topology with their owners. A public example can use explicitly fictional data to explain the mechanism. Such an example is instructional evidence, not an anonymized proof of real public success.

For learning native to the Social Kernel, this repository owns the public generalized method. Editorial, visual, business or research interpretations can follow through their own owners. A distinct lesson belonging to one of those owners returns there directly; Social Kernel need not absorb it.

## Make the public result useful on its own

State what a receiver can now understand or do, where the knowledge acts and what changes in a later non-identical situation. Supply the operating relation, relevant conditions and observable limits. Access to a private originating repository cannot be the hidden prerequisite for an essential public function.

Do not promote a local receipt into universal effectiveness, or reduce an exercised method to an unqualified future idea. Source representation, reachability, discovery, exercise, repeated use and assimilation retain their distinct evidence.

## Return what public use teaches

A new user's context can preserve, alter or reject the transferred relation. Recover the actual difference and return it to the public method, the private source or another owner only where it changes later behavior and the write is selected. Keep their states and authorities separate. Public/private exchange is continued learning rather than automatic synchronization.
