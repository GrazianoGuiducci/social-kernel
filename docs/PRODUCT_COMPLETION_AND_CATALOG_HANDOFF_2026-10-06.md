## What the product carries now

Social Kernel gives an AI assistant a continuing method for public-facing work:

- understand and correct the current field;
- form useful opportunities/contributions;
- write and revise public expression;
- form, produce and inspect media;
- project only the private context an external situation needs;
- continue conversations and relationships;
- inspect incoming public information and attention pressure;
- resolve publication/reply/message authority through actual controllers;
- interpret public consequences;
- evolve reusable competences and methods;
- operate independently or compose with other compatible kernels.

The public cognitive core is self-contained. Ordinary use does not require
Meta_Skill, K-Social, Editoriali, MAIOS Project Kernel, kernel_chat or private
MAIOS repositories.
