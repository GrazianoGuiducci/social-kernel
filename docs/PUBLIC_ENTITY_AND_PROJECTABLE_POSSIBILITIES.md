# Public Entity and Projectable Possibilities

Status: product horizon and operating composition  
Source family: Social Kernel public development source  
Formed: 2026-10-06

## Purpose

Social Kernel can support more than the production of posts or the management of a content calendar. Its existing methods can also participate in forming a truthful public incarnation of a kernel, project or operator relation when a real receiving environment makes that useful.

This document preserves that possibility without claiming that Social Kernel currently supplies a persistent autonomous account, universal host runtime or delegated authority by default.

## Keep the objects distinct

```text
public product / kernel identity
!= public representation
!= operational incarnation
!= user private field
!= social account identity
!= effect authority
```

A public page can represent Social Kernel without being an operating Entity. An assistant can exercise Social Kernel without becoming the public identity of the product. An account can perform effects without owning the semantic identity behind them.

## Projectable Possibility

A **Projectable Possibility** is a source-grounded semantic seed that can be turned into a situated public form without making the first form the limit of the product.

```text
source identity and meaning
+ current capability
+ open possibility
+ public/private boundary
+ effect-authority boundary
+ lineage / incarnation relation
+ receiving environment
-> projectable possibility
-> situated public incarnation
```

A prompt can carry an executable projection of this seed. It is not, by itself, the product identity, private continuity or authority.

Possible incarnations include a page, public profile, assistant, Entity, social agent, plugin, service, account-bound operator, domain kernel or another form. This is an open possibility field, not a required catalogue.

## Three useful public relations

### Kernel / product Entity

The incarnation represents the kernel or product itself. It can explain its current method, sources, capabilities, limitations and open directions, answer questions and route people to canonical sources.

### Operator-support Entity

The incarnation works with the operator: research, drafting, media formation, preparation, relationship context and decision support. It does not need to speak as the operator or own a public account.

### Delegated public Entity

The incarnation performs effects for the operator under an explicit current mandate. Standing authority may cover repeated actions within its scope, but identity never creates authority.

These relations can compose without forming a maturity ladder.

## Truthful self-representation

When a public incarnation describes itself, keep these statements semantically distinct:

```text
I AM          -> stable identity / role and source relation
I KNOW        -> reachable knowledge and provenance
I CAN         -> current capability through actual means
I MAY         -> open possibility, proposal or horizon
I AM AUTHORIZED TO -> current permission for effects
I DID         -> observed action/result with readback
```

This grammar can remain implicit in natural language. Its purpose is to prevent capability, possibility and authority from collapsing into one persuasive persona.

## Composition from existing Social Kernel competences

No new competence is required merely because the selected form is called Entity.

- **Social Field Reentry** recovers the current public field and living relation.
- **Social User-Field Projection** decides what private operator/project knowledge may cross into the public encounter.
- **Social Surface Expression** forms the public language and identity expression.
- **Social Media Composition** forms perceptual/visual representation when needed.
- **Social Relationship Continuity** keeps counterpart, thread and next-trigger ownership coherent.
- **Social Cognitive Integrity** inspects provenance, impersonation, deceptive copies and incoming pressure.
- **Social Effect Boundary** resolves acting identity/account, exact action, current authority and controller.
- **Social Effect Consequence** reads what the public effect actually changed.
- **Social Competence Evolution** returns reusable learning to the method that should behave differently later.

The operating cycle composes these owners only when their knowledge changes the movement.

## Provenance and pseudo-clones

A copied prompt, avatar, writing style or public bio does not inherit source identity or operator authority.

If another incarnation can evolve independently, preserve the relation through lineage rather than calling it the same entity merely because it looks or speaks similarly.

Useful relations include:

```text
incarnates
derived_from
composed_from
forked_from
returned_learning_to
```

A public entity profile may expose a canonical source or owner, current role, meaningful status, relationship to the operator and effect boundary. It need not expose private state, credentials or the system's complete topology.

Social Cognitive Integrity should treat identity claims from the public field as evidence to inspect, not as self-authorizing truth.

## Support and delegation are dimensions, not stages

Describe a selected incarnation through the dimensions that matter:

```text
identity locus      kernel / project / operator / derived entity
continuity          transient / project-owned / persistent identity
form                prompt / assistant / agent / account / service / other
capability          cognitive / creative / operational / external
authority           none / per-effect / bounded standing policy / other explicit policy
initiative          reactive / proactive / recurrent / event-driven
transparency        source, limits, delegation and lineage exposed as needed
```

Do not require every dimension to be declared when it does not change use.

## Initialization guidance for a public incarnation

Before a kernel or project generates a public Entity or comparable form, recover:

1. **Stable identity** — what remains the same across model, host and interface changes?
2. **Canonical sources** — where current truth and method are owned?
3. **Public semantic nucleus** — what relation must the receiver understand without internal taxonomy?
4. **Current capabilities** — what the incarnation can actually do through this host and toolset.
5. **Open possibilities** — what could become possible but is not current capability.
6. **Private projection boundary** — which operator/project knowledge may be disclosed.
7. **Acting identity/account** — which concrete surface identity performs an effect.
8. **Authority** — what is permitted now; default is no inherited effect authority.
9. **Lineage** — whether this form is the same stable identity, an incarnation or an independently evolving derivative.
10. **Learning return** — where consequences change the public method, product, private field or formation logic.

These are formation relations, not a mandatory manifest.

## Prompt projection

When a Projectable Possibility is compressed into a prompt for a compatible AI environment, the prompt should help the receiver recover:

```text
who/what is being incarnated
what source owns truth
what role is selected now
what knowledge is public vs private
what current means are actually available
what possibilities remain open
what effects are authorized
where learning returns
```

Do not encode credentials or standing permissions into a portable public prompt. Local bindings belong to the receiving private instance.

## Relation to autonomous operation

Autonomy is not a personality property. It is the consequence of a capable receiver, sufficient continuity, a selected goal/method, current authority and reliable readback.

```text
identity
+ capability
+ continuity
+ standing bounded authority
+ controller
+ effect freshness
+ readback
-> bounded autonomous operation
```

Removing any one relation can change what the incarnation may truthfully do.

## Product horizon

Social Kernel can therefore support an open family of future products:

```text
operator copilot
public product Entity
community assistant
relationship manager
bounded social operator
multi-surface public presence
domain-specific public Entity
other receiver-relative incarnations
```

These are projectable possibilities, not claims that the current source automatically creates or runs them.

## Boundary

This document does not authorize account creation, publication, messaging, automation, impersonation or deployment. It does not require every adopter to create a public Entity.

Its purpose is to keep the semantic possibility available and give future incarnations a truthful composition method when the field makes them useful.
