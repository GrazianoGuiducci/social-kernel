# Social Kernel Evolution

Evolve the knowledge through which later social work becomes possible. A
source correction, successful encounter, new question, failed handoff or useful
consequence can change that knowledge. The living method should carry the
difference while its source and reason are still understandable.

The [competence-evolution skill](skills/social-competence-evolution/SKILL.md)
conducts this work. [KERNEL](KERNEL.md) supplies source, meaning, temporal
correction and effect boundaries.

## Find what must change

Start from the relation exposed by the work, not from a desired new skill or
folder. Understand the source, the method used or becoming pertinent, what it
preserves, loses or makes possible, and how later work could begin differently.
An emerging intent or newly available resource can expose that relation before
there is an attempted action or an observed result.

Different relations need different continuations:

| Observed relation | Useful continuation |
| --- | --- |
| A current fact, decision or relationship changed | Update its local state and reason. A reusable method need not change. |
| New intent, knowledge, success or a resource makes useful work possible | Let it reform the question and method. Reuse, deepen, compose or form the competence that can carry the continuing possibility. |
| A necessary source or operation was unavailable | Preserve the exact gap and use another authorized means or a bounded handoff. Missing access does not prove missing competence. |
| The competence knew the method but the receiver did not reach it | Repair the actual trigger, entry, reference or routing condition. |
| A body lacks the knowledge needed to handle the situation | Deepen the responsible competence's practical method. |
| The assistant's first framing displaced an available method | Correct the current use and any reusable selection error that would repeat it. |
| A continuing capacity is missing after existing owners compose | Form its smallest useful owner and reachable entry. |
| Several competences now need to coordinate differently | Update each distinct causal contribution and their connection. Preserve their ownership. |

These are ways to understand a real difference, not a mandatory classification
step. Similar wording can hide different causes. Read the body and the
receiving entry when that distinction changes where the correction belongs.

For example, unsuitable copy may result from missing audience knowledge, from
a writer reached only after the theme was fixed, or from a model proposal
recorded as user direction. Adding the same quality checklist to the writer
would address neither of the latter causes.

## Make knowledge operative

Teach why the difference matters and what it changes in perception, source
selection, interpretation, method or action. Retain enough source meaning and
conditions for a later receiver to apply the knowledge in a different case.

A useful competence body lets its receiver understand:

- the purpose and actual object it helps transform;
- the consequential distinctions and source knowledge behind its method;
- how another owner can become pertinent;
- how to detect and correct its own introduced distortion;
- where accumulated learning and continuation remain reachable.

These functions may live in one short skill or in a body with a conditional
guide. Choose the form the knowledge needs. A new title, a rule repeated across
files or an empty template does not add capability.

Make the founding relation operate inside that body's own method. Its audience
model, format or local strategy must remain correctable by the actual source,
user direction and consequences. A central KERNEL link does not compensate for
a skill that fixes the wrong premise before it can be corrected. Teach the
specific choice that changes instead of copying foundation paragraphs into
every body.

A current result can teach more than one owner. A product ambiguity changes
the product explanation; repeated selection of that ambiguous wording can
also teach source-to-expression formation. Return each part where future
behavior must change. General learning about recognition, composition or
formation belongs in this evolution method as well as in the acting
competence when both were causal.

## Learn during the same movement

When a reusable difference is clear, let it change the continuing competence
before the next dependent movement begins. If durable local changes are part
of the selected work, update the owning body and the entries or projections
that must now act differently. Ordinary authorized local edits need no
additional confirmation ritual.

A receipt can preserve the event; it cannot replace this method change.
Conversely, if nothing reusable changed, preserve the result only where
continuation needs it. Do not manufacture a competence update to mark activity.

When another passage is useful, read the changed field from the changed
method. A new source can reform the question or reveal a relation the previous
frame excluded. Repeating the same interpretation is not independent
confirmation. Stop when another passage adds no material difference, and keep
the determination already produced. No fixed number of passes is a runtime
requirement.

If a required source, writable owner or receiving mechanism is unavailable,
keep the current understanding operative and preserve a precise proposed
delta for the owner that can carry it. State the missing capability and the
next observation needed. Do not claim that the remote owner or installed
receiver learned.

## Preserve time and applicability

A correction changes the present method; it does not rewrite earlier
occurrence or manufacture earlier knowledge. Preserve the prior source,
decision and then-available reason when these explain what happened.

An unchanged artifact can acquire a different meaning or eligibility after a
new consequence. Source identity alone therefore cannot establish continued
applicability. Carry the invalidating or qualifying relation with the current
owner so a later instance does not resurrect stale guidance.

When earlier local reasoning was faithful, retain that fact. When it
misrepresented the source, record the correction as such. The two histories
teach different methods. Temporal sequence alone does not establish causality.

## Let the new resultant reach its real consumers

A material change to a kernel, competence, entry, source boundary or proof
forms a new resultant. Identify the consumers that depend on the changed
relation: a skill description, reference, current-state instruction, adapter,
assembled bundle, example or other actual receiving surface.

Reconcile only those that need the difference. Do not propagate by proximity
or require all files to change together. A corrected body beside a stale
trigger can leave the original failure active; a changed adapter alone can
conceal an unchanged source.

Read the resulting consumer, not only the patch plan. Confirm the intended
knowledge is reachable under the circumstance that needs it. If a package is
assembled from the source, inspect the packaged entry and its references as
well as the source body. Source availability and host discovery remain
different facts.

Preserve independently useful contributions from other writers. A newer
timestamp, cleaner checkout or more elaborate explanation does not supersede
their meaning by itself. Recompose the common source, local change and
incoming difference before replacing a body. Keep a conflict explicit if the
relevant source or intent is still unresolved.

## Keep evidence attached to the changed identity

Use checks that can change a concrete claim or protect the actual mechanism.
Their authority remains local to that claim or effect. Uncertainty about a
future release does not prevent understanding, forming a useful method or
preparing work whose current status is explicit.
A parser can establish readable metadata; a path check can establish a
reachable reference; a native surface readback can establish an occurred
effect. Each supports its own relation.

Keep distinct:

- represented knowledge;
- reachable body;
- discoverable entry under the relevant condition;
- exercised behavior;
- changed result;
- learning assimilated in later non-identical use.

A test of revision A is evidence for A. A material revision B needs the checks
whose conclusions could have changed. Several tested components do not prove
that their composed consumer route works. A source author's review does not
become independent receiver evidence.

For a consequential claim, use the smallest observation that could expose a
real disagreement, including a counterexample or a different receiving surface
when useful. A fictional walkthrough explains a method. A mechanical
rehearsal exercises its specified mechanism. Neither proves independent
adoption, real social effectiveness or universal host portability.

Retain regression, tradeoff, unknown and no change where they are the actual
result. New learning can remain useful before stable assimilation is known.

## Preserve private learning and receive upstream change

The public source carries generalized method. The user's private instance
carries local facts, preferences, relationships, permissions and any learned
local method. Their identities and update authority remain distinct.

Before applying an upstream change to a locally evolved method, compare the
prior shared base, the current local knowledge and the incoming relation.
Preserve useful local learning and the conditions that make it valid.
Translate the upstream function into the receiver's actual means; another
host's folders, agents, runtime or authority are not required inheritance.

A local improvement does not automatically modify the public product. A
public update does not automatically authorize replacing local state or
permissions. If the receiver cannot persist or update a needed body, prepare
a readable bounded handoff and describe that limit accurately.

## Generalize only the learning that can travel

When private work exposes a relation useful outside its origin, identify the
part that survives removal of private account, contact, campaign and personal
details. Retain the conditions and evidence state that make it true.

Return Social Kernel method learning to this public source when that
contribution is selected. Distinct editorial, design, business, product or
research learning belongs to the owner that must behave differently.
Public explanations can follow from those sources without becoming their
authority.

Generalizing a private method, writing a contribution, pushing a branch,
releasing a package and installing a receiver are separate effects. Perform
only those covered by the selected work and actual authority. Never put
credentials, raw private conversations or private relationship state into a
public candidate merely to demonstrate provenance.

## Close with a usable continuation

Completion means the selected difference has a coherent living form, its
material consumer paths are reachable, relevant checks apply to the resulting
identity, and the next receiver can recover any consequential uncertainty.

Return the useful work and the method that changed. Include what was actually
exercised and what remains to observe when that affects use. Keep only the
record needed for later work; remove duplicated procedure and stale active
guidance while preserving still-material reasons and history.
